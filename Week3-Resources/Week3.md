**100daysofcybersecurity Week 3 breakdown and resources**  
**Introduction**

Week 3 focuses on building confidence and fluency in the Linux command line. This stage shifts from high level understanding to hands-on system interaction learning how users, files, processes, and permissions actually behave inside a Linux environment. The aim is not just to run commands, but to understand what they do behind the scenes and why they matter, especially from a cybersecurity and system analysis perspective.

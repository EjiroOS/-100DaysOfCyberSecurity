**100daysofcybersecurity Week 2 breakdown and resources**  
**Introduction**

Week Two focuses on moving beyond basic packet capture into real traffic analysis and investigation. The goal is to understand how network communication behaves under normal conditions, identify deviations from that behavior, and analyze packet data the way a network analyst or SOC analyst would.

Throughout this week, I worked on capturing, filtering, and analyzing network traffic using Wireshark, studying how common protocols operate in real environments, and learning how to detect anomalies, suspicious patterns, and abnormal communication flows.
